FORMULA

E<U: T=[1+U²sinh²(κa)/(4E(U−E))]⁻¹; E=U: T=[1+mUa²/(2ℏ²)]⁻¹; E>U: T=[1+U²sin²(qa)/(4E(E−U))]⁻¹

Assumptions

Required: m,E>0 and U,a≥0. The exterior potential is zero on both sides and the mass is identical in all regions. Use one-dimensional stationary nonrelativistic scattering with max(E,U)/(mc²)≤0.01. Here κ=√(2m(U−E))/ℏ and q=√(2m(E−U))/ℏ. The threshold limit and large hyperbolic sine are evaluated stably. Above-barrier phases qa>10¹² are rejected to preserve numerical accuracy. Positive probabilities below the numeric range are reported as errors. Above-barrier resonance inputs that are sensitive to phase-rounding uncertainty are also rejected with a precision error.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
